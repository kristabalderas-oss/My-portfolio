MY EDUCATIONAL BACKGROUND

My journey from elementary school to college

My Academic Journey

Elementary

I finished Grade 1 at Cainta Elementary School. After Grade 1, I transferred to Sua Elementary School, where I continued my elementary education and finished Grade 2 up to Grade 6.

My elementary years were an important part of my journey because they helped me build the foundation for my future studies.

High School

I completed my Junior High School up to Senior High School at Argao National High School. For Senior High School, I took the STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) strand.

My STEM experience helped me develop my interest in technology and prepared me for my college studies.

College

Currently, I am a second-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at Cebu Technological University - Argao Campus.

I chose this program because I want to learn more about technology, programming, and the different fields of Information Technology.

Although studying IT can be challenging, I hope to continue improving my skills and growing as a student.
