About Me

My name is Krista Innova S. Balderas. I am 19 years old and currently residing in Lamacan, Argao.

I am currently a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Cebu Technological University - Argao Campus.

I am the youngest daughter in my family and the last one to complete our little family circle. Being the youngest has taught me to appreciate the love, guidance, and support that my family gives me.

I may be the last daughter, but I carry big dreams and hopes for my future. I enjoy learning, creating, and discovering new things, especially when it comes to technology and creativity.

I believe every experience, whether good or challenging, helps me grow into the person I am meant to be. I hope to become a successful, independent, and kind-hearted woman who can make my family proud.
